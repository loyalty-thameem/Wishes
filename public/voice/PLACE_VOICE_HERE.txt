Optional voice-over (recommended for the hero intro).

Add your recorded voice files here:
- roohi-intro.mp3  (example: "My Roo…hi…")
- roohi-mid.mp3    (example: "As…wi…ya Nus…rath…")
- roohi-end.mp3    (example: "My Roohi… Aswiya Nusrath…")

If you don’t add these files, the site will fall back to the browser’s built-in
speech voice.
